The Collection — Eight Instruments Explained

From legendary American factory craftsmanship to modern artisanal masterpieces, this collection spans the full spectrum of the finest brass engineering in history.

Tenor Trombone · German Boutique
Thein "Belcanto"
Modern Production · Bremen, Germany
Bore.547″ (13.9 mm)
Bell8-1/2″ cut-bell
ValveOpen Flow (Hagmann style)
MaterialYellow Brass (0.45mm wall)

Developed alongside soloist Peter Steiner, the Belcanto bridges the gap between the compact core of standard valves and the free-blowing axial flow. It features a stunning French rim cut-bell design (for travel convenience and a compact core) and is known for its beautiful resonance, secure upper register, and an enticing spectrum of tonal characteristics ranging from sultry lows to projecting highs.

Tenor Trombone · German Boutique
Thein "American 22"
Modern Production · Bremen, Germany
Bore.547″ (13.9 mm)
Bell8-1/2″ French rim
Valve1 Thayer
MaterialYellow Brass (0.5mm wall)

Designed in close cooperation with Nathan Zgonc of the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra. The "American 22" aims to blend Thein's legendary German craftsmanship with the broad, open orchestral sound preferred by American symphonic players. It pairs a traditional Thayer valve with a reversed tuning slide and Belcanto balancer.

Custom Tenor Trombone · Vintage American
Custom Mt. Vernon Bach 42B
1964 Bell / Modern Rebuild
Bell1964 MV Yellow Brass
SlideEdwards w/ 1960 MV Crook
ValveInstrument Innovations
TuningReversed (Unlacquered)

A highly customized "Franken-horn" originally owned by Shachar Israel of the Cleveland Orchestra, rebuilt with extreme precision. It merges a dent-free 1964 Mt. Vernon yellow brass bell, an Instrument Innovations valve block for smooth and quiet action, and an Edwards outer slide paired with a vintage 1960 Mt. Vernon yellow crook. The result is a meaty, responsive, and incredibly colorful representation of the classic Bach sound.

Tenor Trombone · Vintage American
Bach Stradivarius 42G
1970s Corporation Era
Bore.547″ Large Bore
Bell8-1/2″ Gold Brass
ValveTraditional Closed Wrap F
SlideLT (Lightweight)

A highly-prized example from the Vincent Bach "Corporation" era (Elkhart). The Gold Brass (G) bell gives this horn immense resonance and a warm sizzle at high dynamics, while the lightweight slide allows for nimble, clear articulations. Many professional players prefer these carefully selected 1970s Corporation Bachs over modern mass-produced equivalents for their distinctive, broad sound that still anchors the American orchestral trombone section.

Custom Tenor Trombone · Modern Boutique
Stephens Brass Tenor
Modern Production · Vermont, USA
Bore.547″ Large Bore
BellMV Style or Conn 1920s Style
Valve OptionsRotary or Axial Flow
MakerSteve Shires

Built in Vermont by Steve Shires (founder of S.E. Shires Co.), Stephens Brass represents a return to small-batch, fully customized brass making. The tenor models offer a choice between an homage to the Mt. Vernon Bach (yellow/gold brass) or a 1920s Conn style (seamed red brass). Paired with his newly designed rotary valves or an Axial flow, these horns offer an elite, tailored playing experience.

Stephens Brass Bass
Modern Production · Vermont, USA
Bore.562″ or .562/.578″ Dual
ValvesInline F/Gb/D or F/D
Valve TypeStephens Rotary

Another masterwork from Steve Shires, the Stephens Bass is designed to meet the demands of top-tier professionals. It features completely in-house designed rotary valves known for being remarkably airtight and even-slotting. With dual or single bore slide options and wide crooks, the horn creates an immense, structured wall of sound, deeply favored by modern commercial and orchestral bass trombonists.

Jazz Tenor Trombone · American Artisan
Earl Williams Model 6
Mid-20th Century · Burbank, CA
CategorySmall Bore Jazz
Status"Holy Grail" Jazz Horn
MakerEarl Williams
Notable TraitIncredible Upper Register

Referred to as the "Holy Grail" of jazz trombones. Earl Williams crafted instruments out of his shop in Burbank that drew the who's-who of West Coast Trombone Royalty (Dick Nash, Milt Bernhart). The Model 6 is legendary for its vibrant, singing lead voice, extraordinary craftsmanship, and unmatched agility in the upper register. True original Williams horns are extremely rare and coveted artifacts.

Artisan Legend
Alto Trombone · Vintage American
Conn 35H Eb Alto
Elkhart, Indiana
PitchEb Alto
BoreDual Bore
FeatureTuning in Slide
FinishClear Lacquer / Brass

The Conn 35H is a highly distinctive Eb alto trombone famously utilizing a "tuning in slide" mechanism rather than a traditional tuning slide in the bell section. This design preserves the unbroken taper of the bell flare, resulting in a uniquely brilliant and focused alto voice. Vintage Elkhart Conn altos are prized by orchestral players for classical and baroque repertoire.
